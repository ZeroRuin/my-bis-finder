My BiS Finder 0.9.5 — local verification

Open the project in IntelliJ. Disable the installed My BiS Finder, then run the
Gradle run task and enable My BiS Finder.

1. Scan the bank, choose a target, generate and select a combat style.
2. Right-click a sidebar gear icon and choose Exclude from My BiS Finder.
3. Confirm icons, stats and the BiS bank view remain visible. The warning says:
   Exclusion list updated. Generate again to apply changes.
4. Exclude several items without regenerating. Switch combat styles; the old
   results should remain available and the warning should remain visible.
5. In the BiS bank view, exclude a recommended rune, food and potion. Check
   normal withdrawal actions still work. Other items must have no exclude action.
6. Regenerate once. Excluded gear and supplies must no longer be recommended;
   supported owned food/potion alternatives should be chosen when available.
   If a required rune is excluded, a spell using it must not be selected unless
   an allowed weapon/tome supplies that rune. Charged powered staves retain
   their built-in attacks; excluding loose runes does not remove stored charges.
7. Settings > My BiS Finder > Excluded items: delete individual
   names, then regenerate to restore them. Ctrl+A and Delete clears the whole list.
8. Exclude one potion dose and confirm the other doses are excluded too.
9. Restart and confirm the list persists in the RuneLite configuration profile.
10. On a GIM account, check exclusions in both storage views and the reference
    icons. An exclusion applies to the same item in every ownership source.
11. Ordinary bank menus and unrelated items must have no plugin exclusion option.

Automated checks: mechanicsSelfTest, gimStorageSelfTest, itemExclusionSelfTest,
loadoutStatsSizingSelfTest.
These checks can be run locally before submitting the update to Plugin Hub.

12. Select Magic with a long spell name (such as Dark Demonbane + Mark of
    Darkness). Confirm Prayer Bonus remains visible. Switch to shorter styles
    and confirm the stats box shrinks again. Resize the sidebar/client and check
    wrapping and the outer sidebar scroll still work.
